Hello, Tesseract

The first working piece of AI PANORAMA: a genuinely four-dimensional space you can turn with your own hands.

AI PANORAMA is going to be a free encyclopedia-magazine about artificial intelligence that you can walk around inside. Not a news feed that scrolls away, but a whole picture you can visit on any day.

This page is not the magazine yet. There is no real content here at all - every node you see is a placeholder with a made-up name. What is real is the hard part: the fourth dimension. If that works, everything else is ordinary work. So it was built first.

Open on this screen Mouse and keyboard. Works on any laptop or desktop. Open in Virtual Reality Meta Quest 3 and other WebXR headsets. Open this page in the headset's own browser, then press Enter VR.

What you are looking for

A tesseract is to a cube what a cube is to a square. A square has four corners, a cube has eight, a tesseract has sixteen. You cannot see one properly, in the same way a drawing on paper cannot properly show a cube - but you can watch one turn, and that is the part your brain can actually learn.

Turn it in the ZW plane and watch it pass through itself and come out inside-out. Turn it four times and it comes exactly home. Nothing is broken when that happens. That is what four dimensions look like.

On a screen: what to press

  • Drag with the mouse to turn it the ordinary way.
  • Tab chooses which hyper-plane you are working in: XW, YW or ZW.
  • Shift and drag turns it through the fourth dimension.
  • Shift and the left or right arrow snaps a quarter turn. Four snaps come home.
  • W and S swim the slab from fresh news toward settled knowledge.
  • E switches between the calm slice view and the whole structure at once.
  • Home puts everything back. You can never get lost.

In the headset: what to press

  • Right thumbstick turns it the ordinary way.
  • Click the left thumbstick to choose the hyper-plane. The little instrument on your left forearm shows which one.
  • Push the left thumbstick forwards or back to turn through the fourth dimension. Flick it sideways to snap a quarter turn.
  • Hold the left trigger and push the stick to swim from news to encyclopedia.
  • X switches slice and whole-structure views. Y opens the menu at your hand, where Undo and Reset always live in the first two places.
  • Squeeze one hand to move the whole thing. Squeeze both and pull apart to make it bigger.

Comfort

The room never moves and you are never moved. Only the object turns, and it turns slowly on purpose. If anything feels uncomfortable for even a moment, that is a bug worth reporting, not something to push through.
