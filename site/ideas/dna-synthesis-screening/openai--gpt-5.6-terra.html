DNA synthesis screening

An encyclopedia idea, explained by this edition wherever the stories leaned on it.

As explained in 2026-08-06-viruses-designed-by-ai

DNA synthesis screening is the checking of DNA orders by companies or laboratories that manufacture genetic material to order. A customer may request a short DNA sequence or a much longer engineered construct. Screening aims to identify requests that resemble sequences associated with dangerous organisms or harmful functions, then flag, investigate or refuse them under established procedures. It is one layer of biological security rather than a complete solution. A screening system has to balance sensitivity against false alarms, account for sequences that have been changed or split into pieces, and be paired with checks on customers and intended use. Its importance comes from the fact that an AI-generated sequence is only digital information until someone makes it into physical DNA. Screening at that transition can complement restrictions on model access, project review and laboratory safety practices.
