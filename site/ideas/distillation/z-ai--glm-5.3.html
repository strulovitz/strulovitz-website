Distillation

An encyclopedia idea, explained by this edition wherever the stories leaned on it.

As explained in 2026-07-17-kimi-k3

Distillation is a way of training one AI model using another model's behaviour as the teaching material. Instead of learning only from human-written text, the student model is fed large volumes of a teacher model's answers and learns to reproduce the patterns behind them. The technique is old, respectable and widely used — most often to compress a big model's ability into a small, cheap one, which is why small models sometimes punch above their size. The controversy arises when the teacher's owner did not permit this use: several leading AI companies forbid training competing models on their outputs, and have accused rivals, particularly Chinese laboratories, of doing exactly that. The accused generally answer that the complainants built their own models on text scraped from the public internet without asking every author. Whether learning from a model is meaningfully different from learning from the internet is an unresolved argument at the centre of current AI politics.
