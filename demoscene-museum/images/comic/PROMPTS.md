# The Real-Time Machine — image prompts (13 comic pages)

HOW TO USE: for every page, paste the COMMON BLOCK first, then that page's
prompt, as ONE message to the image model.  Generate portrait 2:3 (e.g.
1024x1536 or larger).  Save as images/comic/page-NN.webp, 1200x1800,
WebP quality 82.  The model has no memory, which is why the common block
describes the style, the three characters and the machine every time.

======================================================================
COMMON BLOCK — paste at the start of EVERY prompt
======================================================================

A full comic-book page in portrait format (2:3), with a clear grid of
panels separated by white gutters and bold black panel borders.  ART
STYLE: the CHARACTERS are drawn in the spirit of classic Franco-Belgian
comic albums ("ligne claire"): clean, even black outlines, flat colours
with soft cel shading, expressive rounded faces, slightly exaggerated
noses and gestures, lively body language, warm and witty.  The
BACKGROUNDS are drawn in the spirit of late-1980s/1990s Japanese
cyberpunk anime: hand-painted cel-style cityscapes and interiors, dense
detail, dramatic perspective, rain, neon in teal, magenta and gold, CRT
monitors, thick cables, holographic adverts showing only abstract glyphs.
Speech bubbles are white with black outlines; the dialogue is lettered in
clean, legible ALL-CAPS comic lettering EXACTLY as written below, and
there is NO other text anywhere except what is specified.  Caption boxes
are pale yellow rectangles.  Printed-paper feel with subtle halftone.
Mature, smart, warm humour — not childish.  No real brand names, no real
logos, no existing copyrighted characters.

THE CAST (always drawn exactly like this):

LÉA, 42, a woman of average height, athletic.  Short dark-brown bob with
one dyed teal streak at the front, freckles, sharp amused eyes.  Wears an
oversized black hoodie with a small pattern of tiny coloured squares on
the chest, dark cargo trousers, white sneakers, a chunky black digital
watch.  She is a programmer: calm, dry, quick.

BENNY, 45, a tall lanky man with a big friendly nose, curly ginger hair
greying at the temples, round wire glasses, a three-day beard.  Wears a
worn olive-green 1990s bomber jacket covered in small embroidered
patches of abstract geometric designs, a faded black t-shirt, blue
jeans, and big black headphones always around his neck.  He is a
musician: dramatic, nostalgic, exaggerates everything.

NOA, 18, a young woman, short and sporty.  Hair in two buns dyed violet,
a small silver nose ring, a cropped yellow puffer jacket over a black
top, baggy grey trousers, chunky white trainers.  A smartphone is always
in one hand and a skateboard is often under her arm.  She is Léa's
niece: sarcastic, bored at first, secretly curious.

THE TIME MACHINE: a beige mid-1990s desktop tower PC enlarged to the
size of a wardrobe, standing upright, with a round red TURBO button the
size of a dinner plate on its front, a flip-up bulging CRT monitor on
top, a 5.25-inch floppy-disk slot big enough to walk into, and a tangle
of thick grey cables.  When active it glows teal from every vent.

with abstract glyphs, steam from a grate.  Centre: the TIME MACHINE,

At the very bottom, small lettering: "DEMOSCENE MUSEUM".

PAGE 4 — 1991, THE CRACKTRO
======================================================================

Five panels.  Setting: a teenager's bedroom in 1991 reimagined with a

with copper pipes and small pressure gauges, pneumatic tubes carrying
floppy disks across the ceiling, a gas lamp glow, a mattress on the

sine-shaped line of scrolling text made of ABSTRACT UNREADABLE marks,
plus a bouncing red-and-white checkered ball.

PANEL 1 (wide, top): the room; a skinny teenager with wild curly ginger
hair (young Benny), back to camera, at the machine.  The three stand
behind him unseen.  BENNY, hand over mouth: "THAT'S ME. LOOK AT THAT
HAIR."  LÉA: "WE'RE LOOKING. WE CAN'T STOP."

PANEL 2: close on the screen with the rainbow bars and wavy scroller.

PANEL 3: LÉA holding a floppy disk that just arrived through a brass

PANEL 5 (bottom, wide): NOA leaning close to the screen, rainbow bars
