ABOUT NIR

As a boy I stood in front of arcade machines and never put in a coin. I
watched the machine play by itself and asked one question: how do they do
that?

Later I worked as a programmer in the Israeli army and at Elbit, and I learned
from Andre LaMothe's game-programming books and from a Dr. Dobb's Journal
issue on graphics programming.

To this day I am bad at playing games, and I enjoy them completely -- because
I am looking at how they are made, not at how to win.

That is what this site is: one question asked in front of a screen, answered
with school ideas, for the teenager who is told in class that he is not good
at this.
