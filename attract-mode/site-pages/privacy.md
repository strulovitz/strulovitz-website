PRIVACY

An account belongs to an adult. The first name is a nickname for your
teenager; the surname can be one letter.

WHAT IS KEPT
- An email address, for signing in and for the password letter.
- The nickname.
- The scores of the Challenges that were played.

WHAT IS NOT KEPT
- No advertising, and no advertising profiles.
- No messages, no forums, no comments: there is nowhere on this site to write
  to another visitor.
- Nobody who bought can see who else bought, and profiles are not public.
- No outside analytics. A page may ask for one tiny file that records only
  which page was reached and when.

WHO CAN CONTACT WHOM
All contact goes to the adult's email, through the contact address on this
site: [CONTACT ADDRESS -- PENDING NIR].

DELETING AN ACCOUNT
Write to the contact address and say you want the account deleted. It is
deleted with Moodle's own tool, by hand, not by a robot.

CO-OP RECORDINGS
A Co-op hour is recorded, with your yes in the email and again aloud at the
start. The customer receives a copy. If the learner is a minor, a parent is
in the room, and the hour is booked and paid by an adult.

Nothing here is sold, and no data is given to anyone.
