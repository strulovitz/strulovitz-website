TASK CARDS (Part 11, section 11.8)

One card, one result, one way to prove it. A card names its role and the
sections to read. The next agent takes ONE card, reads what it names, and
does it.

THE GOLDEN CARD is 0-00-golden-card.md: it is the example printed in
Part 11, section 11.3, copied word for word. Every new card is made by
copying it and changing the fields.

THE CARDS OF STAGE 0 are 0-01.md to 0-15.md. Their state says the truth of
2026-11-28, not a wish:
  done            the result exists and can be pointed at
  open            the result does not exist yet
  waiting-for-nir it needs one of Nir's owner acts (Part 01, section 1.12)

"am task next ROLE" will print the next open card of a role (Part 11,
section 11.12, item 2). Until that command exists, read the cards.
