An artificial intelligence researcher who worked at Anthropic — and previously OpenAI — has resigned, saying both tech giants are "gambling with our lives."
Jacob Coxon shared his exit in a post on X, warning that the world should "not underestimate the power of this technology." He added: "These will soon be superhuman systems that can hack anything, revolutionize any field overnight, and acquire real power and resources. We have all witnessed the progress in each of these domains, and progress is not slowing."
Coxon said both Anthropic and OpenAI are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence," which refers to a scenario in which AI models can develop a more capable successor of themselves, creating an unstoppable feedback loop.
