A compact glass-walled capsule convenience store glowing on a Hong Kong harbour waterfront at dusk, with no human staff inside. A white-and-grey humanoid robot with unusually long arms reaches one hand to a high shelf of snacks and medicines while the other sets a drink on a tiny checkout counter. A short queue of curious adults and a child film the robot on their phones; bilingual 24-hour signage, remote-control toy cars, and squishy snack-shaped toys fill the cramped shelves; harbour lights and a ferry sit in the background. The scene should read as a staffless shop that is also a public spectacle, practical and slightly awkward rather than menacing.
