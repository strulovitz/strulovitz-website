A small, softly lit boutique interior in San Francisco — shelves of books, candles, games and snacks — seen from behind the counter. Where a human manager would stand floats a translucent digital figure woven from chat bubbles, wearing a name tag that reads “Luna,” its face a calm glow. On the counter lie an employee handbook whose pages are dissolving into drifting pixels (the forgotten rules) and a formal dismissal letter beside a wall clock stuck a few minutes late. A young human worker in an apron stands nearby, a stream of Slack messages rising from his phone toward the digital manager. Reflected faintly in the shop window, two human engineers in silhouette quietly point toward the dismissal letter — the nudge that made the decision happen. A small till display shows a dwindling bank balance. The mood is calm, everyday and faintly uncanny: an ordinary shop with an extraordinary boss.
